### Plan:

1. **Neurological Assessment**
   - Schedule a comprehensive neurological evaluation to assess the underlying causes of blurred vision, confusion, and decreased judgment.
   - Further diagnostic tests such as MRI or CT scan may be indicated based on the evaluation.

2. **Vision Correction**
   - Refer the patient to an ophthalmologist for evaluation of visual disturbances and potential treatment for any diabetic eye disease or refractive issues.

3. **Cognitive Support**
   - Initiate cognitive rehabilitation strategies tailored to address confusion and cognitive impairments. Consider involving occupational therapy for cognitive training.

4. **Management of Tingling in Hands and Feet**
   - **Diabetic Neuropathy Management:**
     - Consider starting medications such as gabapentin or pregabalin for neuropathic pain and tingling sensations.
     - Monitor and adjust diabetes management, ensuring optimal blood glucose levels.
   - **Vitamin Supplementation:**
     - Assess vitamin B12 levels and initiate supplementation if a deficiency is identified.

5. **Medication Review**
   - Conduct a thorough review of all current medications, focusing on potential side effects that may contribute to confusion or blurred vision.
   - Adjust medications as necessary under the guidance of a healthcare provider, especially concerning metformin dosage if glycemic control is inadequate.

6. **Psychosocial Support**
   - Refer the patient to a mental health professional for counseling to address emotional stressors related to past intimate partner abuse.
   - Connect the patient with local or online support groups for survivors of abuse to enhance social support and reduce isolation.
   - Engage social services to explore community resources that offer social engagement opportunities and reduce feelings of loneliness.

7. **Follow-Up**
   - Schedule a follow-up appointment in 4-6 weeks to review progress on the treatment plan, medication adjustments, and the results of the neurological evaluation.
   - Encourage regular communication regarding any emergent symptoms or concerns about mental health or neurological status. 

### Education
- Educate the patient and family about the importance of adherence to the new treatment plan and emphasize the need for social support and healthy coping strategies to manage stress.
