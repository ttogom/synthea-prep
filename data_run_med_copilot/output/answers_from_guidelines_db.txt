### Diagnostic Criteria for Gingival Disease
- **Historical Evaluation**:
  - Review past dental records for documented instances of gingivitis or periodontal disease.
  - Assess for symptoms such as bleeding gums, swelling, and pocket depth measurements during dental examinations.
- **Current Assessment**:
  - Use clinical indices such as the Gingival Index (GI) and Periodontal Index (PI) to evaluate the severity of gingival inflammation.
  - Measure probing depth and attachment loss to determine the extent of periodontal disease.
  - Radiographic evaluation may be necessary to assess bone loss associated with periodontal disease.

### Investigations for Recurrent Cystitis
- **Urinalysis and Culture**:
  - Perform a urinalysis to check for leukocytes, nitrites, and blood.
  - Urine culture to identify specific pathogens and their antibiotic sensitivities.
- **Imaging Studies**:
  - Consider renal ultrasound or CT scan to rule out anatomical abnormalities or obstructions.
- **Further Testing**:
  - Evaluate for underlying conditions such as diabetes or urinary tract abnormalities that may predispose to recurrent infections.

### Management Strategies for Dental Health Issues
- **Gingival Disease**:
  - Recommend improved oral hygiene practices, including regular brushing and flossing.
  - Professional dental cleanings to remove plaque and tartar buildup.
  - Consider antimicrobial mouth rinses or topical treatments for localized inflammation.
- **Loose Dental Fillings**:
  - Assess the condition of existing fillings; if loose, they may need to be replaced or repaired.
  - Discuss the potential need for restorative treatments to address any decay or structural issues in the teeth.

### Mental Health Screenings and Assessments
- **Screening Tools**:
  - Utilize standardized questionnaires such as the Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9) for depression and the Generalized Anxiety Disorder 7-item scale (GAD-7) for anxiety.
- **Comprehensive Assessment**:
  - Conduct a thorough psychosocial evaluation to identify stressors, coping mechanisms, and support systems.
  - Consider referrals to mental health professionals if significant concerns are identified during screenings.
- **Follow-Up**:
  - Regular follow-up assessments to monitor mental health status and adjust interventions as necessary.
