### Recommended Interventions for Blurred Vision and Neurological Involvement
- **Referral to Specialists**: 
  - An ophthalmologist should be consulted for a comprehensive eye examination to assess the cause of blurred vision.
  - A neurologist referral is essential to evaluate potential neurological conditions that may be contributing to visual disturbances.
- **Neurological Assessment**: 
  - A thorough neurological assessment should be performed to identify any underlying neurological disorders that could be causing the symptoms.

### Cognitive Assessments for Confusion and Decreased Judgment
- **Cognitive Testing**: 
  - Conduct a formal cognitive assessment, which may include:
    - Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) or Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) to evaluate overall cognitive function.
    - Specific tests for memory, attention, and executive function to pinpoint areas of impairment.
- **Neuropsychological Evaluation**: 
  - Consider referral for a comprehensive neuropsychological evaluation if initial assessments indicate significant cognitive impairment.

### Medications or Treatments for Tingling in Hands and Feet
- **Potential Treatments**:
  - **Vitamin B12 Supplementation**: If deficiency is suspected, as it can lead to peripheral neuropathy and cognitive issues.
  - **Neuropathic Pain Medications**: Consider medications such as gabapentin or pregabalin, which are effective for neuropathic pain and tingling sensations.
- **Management of Underlying Conditions**: 
  - Address any underlying conditions (e.g., diabetes, vitamin deficiencies) that may be contributing to neurological decline and peripheral neuropathy.

### Integration of Stress Management Techniques
- **Stress Management Techniques**:
  - Incorporate mindfulness practices, such as meditation or yoga, to help reduce stress and improve cognitive function.
  - Encourage participation in social activities or support groups to combat feelings of isolation and enhance social interaction.
- **Psychological Support**: 
  - Consider referral to a psychologist or counselor for cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) to address anxiety and confusion related to cognitive decline. 

By addressing these areas, the patient's overall care plan can be more comprehensive, targeting both neurological and cognitive symptoms effectively.
